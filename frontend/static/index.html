BOUNDARY

What the data cannot prove

The public sources do not directly measure each NBFC's internal analytics-system adoption, borrower-level approval quality, repayment, default, processing time or profitability. The model therefore supports descriptive and predictive research analysis—not a causal claim about analytics driving disbursement.
